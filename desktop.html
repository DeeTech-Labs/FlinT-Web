Connected

From the first connection.

Discover devices over USB and Bluetooth LE, inspect device status and synchronize time from your PC. Settings, profiles, backup and restore, built-in face selection and Face Studio require compatible protocol v1 firmware.

  • USB & Bluetooth LE
  • PC time synchronization
  • Settings & profiles
  • Faces & Face Studio
  • Session capture & JSON export

Availability

Built in the open.

Desktop is in alpha. Linux builds and the USB client are covered by CI. Windows and macOS are target platforms; validated packages are not yet available. Firmware updates, Actions and Android remain in development.
