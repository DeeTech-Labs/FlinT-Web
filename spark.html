01 / FLINT DEVICE

Meet Spark.

The first FlinT device. A compact clock and display, brought to life by FlinT OS.

Build the firmwareConnect with Desktop

Hardware

Small board. Room to create.

Spark combines an ESP32-C3 with an ST7789 240 × 280 display and USB Serial/JTAG. The hardware profile and wiring live alongside the firmware.

  • ESP32-C3
  • ST7789 · 240 × 280
  • USB Serial/JTAG
  • FlinT OS board profile

Getting started

Bring Spark to life.

Build and flash FlinT OS using the documented Spark board configuration, then connect Desktop to synchronize the clock and configure your device. Bluetooth LE requires a compatible NimBLE firmware build.
